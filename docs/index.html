Features

Everything you need to manage COPD

Built specifically for COPD patients — track what matters, share what you choose, and stay ahead of exacerbations.

Oxygen & SpO2 Tracking

Log and monitor your blood oxygen levels over time. Spot trends and share readings with your doctor.

Medication Management

Track daily and exacerbation medications, mark them as discontinued, and keep a complete history.

Nutrition & Hydration

Log meals, track macros and micronutrients, and monitor daily water intake for better respiratory health.

Doctor Portal

Share your health data with your care team securely. Your doctor sees exactly what you choose to share.

Exercise Tracking

Log pulmonary rehab and daily activity. Monitor steps and heart rate with Health Connect integration.

Exacerbation Tools

Track symptoms and triggers, save notes to discuss with your clinician, and access educational resources.

How it works

Simple. Secure. Built for you.

Getting started takes minutes. No complicated setup — just download, create your profile, and start tracking.

1

Download the app

Available on Android. Get COPD Fuel on Google Play, install it, and create your secure account in under two minutes.

2

Set up your profile

Add your medications, your care team, and health goals. Everything stays private unless you choose to share.

3

Track every day

Log oxygen, meals, exercise, and symptoms. The app builds your health picture over time.

4

Share with your doctor

When you're ready, share your data with your care team through the secure doctor portal.
